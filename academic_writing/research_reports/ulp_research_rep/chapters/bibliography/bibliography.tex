\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{cap:Literature_Review}

The theoretical foundation of this work involves concepts from different areas
of Mathematics and Computer Science. In particular, the literature review is
organized around four main topics: numerical methods, error analysis,
floating-point arithmetic, and programming paradigms.

These topics are directly related within the context of this research.
Numerical methods provide the mathematical algorithms used in the experiments;
error analysis provides the theoretical framework required to assess the
quality of numerical approximations; floating-point arithmetic establishes the
limitations of numerical representation in digital computers; and the study of
programming paradigms provides the conceptual basis for analyzing different
ways of implementing numerical algorithms
\cite{burden2016numerical,chapra2021numerical,higham2002accuracy}.
